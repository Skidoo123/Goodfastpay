Help Center FAQs

How long do gift card reviews take?

Generally, gift card reviews are completed within 10 to 30 minutes depending on the card brand. You will receive an instant notification in your security logs when the review updates.

What are the withdrawal processing times?

Withdrawals are processed instantly upon validation. If your bank network is experiencing delays, it can take up to 2 hours for standard settlements to post.

How do I set up my Transaction PIN?

You can configure or update your secure 4-digit Transaction PIN directly from the Settings / Bank Details tab in the sidebar navigation menu.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal amount permitted on the platform is ₦1,000. Maximum limits vary based on account activity levels.
